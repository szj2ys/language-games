1. **[E] pupil/levels.ts › p-5 (lines 161, 173, 181).** The explanation the pupil reads as the winning way (`looks-ahead`) is ▶ "Verbs about the future (want, plan, hope) take “to”; most others take -ing." Both halves are false. Many other verbs take "to" (seem, manage, pretend, tend, happen), and many verbs about the future take -ing (avoid, consider, suggest, look forward to). The exam's own "avoid" is as much about the future as "hope", yet its answer is ▶ "avoid swimming". A learner who trusts the rule writes "avoid to swim", a common learner error. The way's label (line 161) repeats the error: "-ing after the others". Separately, the deck card ▶ "mind swimming" models affirmative "mind", which is rare; the verb is used in "Do you mind…?" and "I don't mind…".
   Replace:
   - the `explain[1]` text: "Verbs of wanting and planning (want, hope, decide, plan, promise) take “to”. Enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, practise and keep take -ing: learn them as a list."
   - the `looks-ahead` label: "“to” after want, hope, plan…; -ing after enjoy, finish, avoid…"
   - the deck item: `patternItem({ word: "don't mind", looksAhead: false })`. `patternItem` (engine.ts:177) then gives "don't mind swimming", with "don't mind to swim" as the slip.
