1. **Major — zero is wrongly denied a measurement use.** `src/games/family/rounds.ts:111`: linking whole numbers → zero with “can measure an amount” is rejected. As a learner, I can defend “zero litres.” **Fix:** add `measure` to zero's features. [Phone evidence](../qa/playtest-3/phone-family-zero-measure-rejected.png).

2. **Major — number values and their uses are graded as if interchangeable.** `src/games/family/rounds.ts:98`, `:108`, `:112`, `:211`: “All … can be added together” rejects bus numbers, although 42 + 7 is valid arithmetic; the shirt round says whole numbers cannot name things, although shirt 7 does exactly that. **Fix:** label cards as uses (“counting apples”, “identifying a bus route”) and ask about what the numbers **do here**, replacing the bus hint with “Adding route labels does not tell you how many buses there are.” [Addition rejection](../qa/playtest-3/phone-family-1-all-wrong.png), [naming rejection](../qa/playtest-3/phone-family-whole-name-rejected.png).

3. **Major — the accepted -ing explanation omits its own exceptions.** `src/games/pupil/levels.ts:303` says to double a final consonant after a single vowel in a stressed syllable, without excluding w/x/y. Yet `engine.ts:158` excludes them, and the displayed test accepts **playing**. **Fix:** add “Do not double w, x or y” to the explanation and summary at `levels.ts:278`. [Displayed rule and test](../qa/playtest-3/phone-pupil-8-explain-wrong.png); [spelling reference](https://sites.google.com/education.vic.gov.au/phonograms/stage-4/rule-9).

4. **Major — §67 is presented as a history it does not give.** `src/content/sources.ts:162` claims fractions earned their name through measuring, negatives through addition, and bus numbers connect only through digits. Anscombe's §67 proposes overlapping relationships, not those historical causes or that exclusive connection. **Fix:** describe these as this game's comparisons: “Fractions and whole numbers can measure amounts; negative and whole numbers can be added; route numbers identify buses.” [Rendered gloss](../qa/playtest-3/phone-family-source.png); [Anscombe §67](https://manuals.plus/m/d1732a8b85530469e1e27b7031baaab81c11e9452f00cc5c6b7a8207fd81d4bc.pdf#page=20).

5. **Major — the pupil's twist explanations overclaim their citations.** `src/games/pupil/levels.ts:271` uses §217 to explain acquisition of irregular forms; that passage concerns justification for following rules. At `:312`, §§198–201 do not make hearing a prerequisite for spelling. **Fix:** teach “have → has; be → is” and “keep -ee: see → seeing; drop silent e: make → making”; separately label the practice analogy. Use word-only `see → seing`, avoiding the awkward context-free “I am seeing it.” [p-7 feedback](../qa/playtest-3/phone-pupil-7-wrong-twist.png), [p-8 feedback](../qa/playtest-3/phone-pupil-8-wrong-twist.png); [Anscombe §§198–201](https://manuals.plus/m/d1732a8b85530469e1e27b7031baaab81c11e9452f00cc5c6b7a8207fd81d4bc.pdf#page=44), [§217](https://manuals.plus/m/d1732a8b85530469e1e27b7031baaab81c11e9452f00cc5c6b7a8207fd81d4bc.pdf#page=46).
