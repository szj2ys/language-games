b4-5|Two stone slabs lie on the ground, one big and one small, side by side; the builder in a cobalt jacket waves vaguely towards both of them; the assistant in a sage apron looks from one to the other, puzzled.
b4-6|A wooden delivery cart loaded with two stone beams has just arrived in the yard; the stock-keeper (woman with short grey hair, ochre jacket) reads a delivery note aloud; the assistant in a sage apron holds a stock-list sheet and a pencil.
